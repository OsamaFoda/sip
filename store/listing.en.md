# Sip — Data Saver: AMO listing (English)

**Name:** Sip — Data Saver
(Before submitting, search addons.mozilla.org for "Sip" to check the name doesn't clash.)

**Category:** Privacy & Security

**Summary** (under 250 characters):
See how much data each site uses, keep to your monthly plan, and stop the waste: autoplaying videos, heavy trackers and more. YouTube quality is yours to choose. Everything stays on your device.

**Description:**

Sip is for anyone on a capped data plan. It counts what this browser uses, site by site, warns you before you run out, and saves data where it can.

What's left, at a glance
- How much of your plan is left and how many days until it renews, with a bar that shows where your current pace ends.
- Today's fair share of what's left, and a warning in amber when you go over it.
- The sites that used the most, today or this period.
- Warnings at 75%, 90% and 100%, and once a week if you're on pace to run out early.
- Line it up with your carrier: enter what they say you've used, or what's left, and Sip counts on from there. A 5% estimate covers the network overhead a browser never sees (you can change it).

Saving levels, in one click
- Light blocks heavy trackers. Pages look and play as usual.
- Balanced (the default) also stops videos from playing and downloading on their own.
- Max also blocks images. Pages look unfinished; turn images back on for the sites you need.
Each saver says what it saves and what it may break, and you can set each one yourself, or per site.

What is on from the first minute
These change how some sites behave, so you should know: Sip starts on Balanced, which stops autoplaying video and blocks heavy trackers, and it limits YouTube to 480p. YouTube videos you open still play. Choose another level, or another YouTube quality (240p to 1080p, or no limit), any time.

YouTube quality is your choice
Saving levels never change it. Lower it and the video playing now follows; raise it and it applies from the next video.

Every saver and what it may break
- Stop autoplay: videos and sound don't play or download until you press play. Some players need a second click.
- Block heavy trackers: Sip's own short list of ad and tracking hosts that load on page after page. Rarely breaks anything, though "Log in with Facebook" buttons may not work. For much more blocking, use uBlock Origin alongside Sip.
- Block images: the biggest saving. Pages look unfinished.
- Block web fonts: font files a site downloads on your first visit. Icons may turn into words like "menu".
- Ask sites for lighter pages (Save-Data): off by default. Few sites honour it, and it makes your browser a little easier to tell apart.

Nothing leaves your device
Sip makes no network requests of its own and collects nothing. Your history stays in your browser for two months, and you can delete it any time.

In English and Arabic, light and dark.
