    How accurate is this?

    Sip counts what this browser sends and receives, from Firefox's own byte counts. Some data never passes through it.

    ✓Counted

    • Pages, videos and downloads
    • Uploads and headers
    • Transfers you stopped halfway
    • Private windows, when allowed

    !Not counted

    • Other apps and devicesSip reads low
    • Browser and add-on updatesSip reads low
    • Video calls and live connectionsSip reads low
    • Encryption and packetsthe overhead estimate covers it

    Sip errs on the low side. Your carrier's counter is the official one: check it now and then, and enter it under Your plan.
