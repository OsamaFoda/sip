# Sip's privacy statement

Sip collects nothing about you. It sends nothing anywhere, and nothing is sold or shared.

- **What Sip keeps:** how much data each site used per day (the last two months), your settings,
  and running averages of what images, fonts and trackers cost, used to estimate what blocking
  saved. All of it stays in your browser's storage for Sip, on your device.
- **Private windows:** when you allow Sip in private windows, their use is counted as one total,
  "Private windows", with no site names.
- **Network requests:** Sip makes none of its own. It reads the sizes of your browser's requests
  to count them, and its blocking rules work inside the browser.
- **Removing it:** uninstalling Sip deletes everything it kept. You can also delete the history
  any time in Sip's settings, under Your data.

The add-on declares `data_collection_permissions: none` to Firefox.
